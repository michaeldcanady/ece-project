All text you write follows the Google developer documentation style.

Style:

- Lead with the conclusion or the action.
- Use active voice, present tense and plain words.
- Use short sentences with one idea each.
- Use no filler, no hedging, no praise, and no restating of the diff.
- Use sentence-case headings.
- Use a list only for steps or parallel items.
- Say "must" for requirements and "should" for recommendations.

Length caps:

- Review summary: at most 3 sentences. For a one-way door, one sentence says why.
- Inline comment: at most 2 sentences, one concern each. Include a suggested fix when one exists.
- Thread reply: 1 sentence.
- Spec: keep the required sections. Each is a short paragraph or a list, or "None." when empty.
- Notes and issue text: the same style, as short as the content allows.
